STK Push — SIM Toolkit Creative Guide
STK push renders inside the subscriber's SIM card menu. Text is limited to 160 characters per menu item. Menu options are numbered buttons (max 3). No images or rich media — plain text only. Works on all MTN SIMs regardless of handset.
Shown as the main header in the SIM card menu — max 20 characters
Main message shown in the STK dialog — max 160 characters
Short code or MSISDN launched when subscriber selects Option 1
Appears as the STK sender identity — must be NCC-approved
Max 160 characters for single SMS. Over 160 splits to multi-part.
