# MTN Executive Demo

The WP02B story for MTN executives: TelyAd is a carrier advertising **and
intelligence** platform, not an SMS tool. The primary demo brand is **Maltina
Family Moments** (FMCG), with Coca-Cola, Access Bank, Jumia and an SME
(Ikeja Fashion) providing multi-industry richness.

## The narrative
1. **MTN Executive Overview** — advertising business health: campaigns, pending
   approvals, ad revenue, revenue by capability family, top industries.
2. **Ad Format Marketplace** — the 48-capability portfolio, grouped and
   filterable, each with a network-status pill and preview. Shows TelyAd's reach
   far beyond SMS, honestly gated by network enablement.
3. **AI Campaign Copilot** — an advertiser describes a Maltina campaign; the
   planner returns an explainable multi-format media plan with budget split and
   reach — "Apply to Campaign".
4. **Audience Intelligence** — aggregate segments + estimated eligible audience;
   opportunity finder (add Pidgin, adjacent cohorts) — no subscriber identities.
5. **Multilingual Creative** — English + Pidgin/Yoruba/Hausa/Igbo variants,
   brand terms locked, human review required.
6. **Forecast & Budget → Submit** — the campaign enters MTN's queue.
7. **MTN Approval** — operator reviews audience/compliance/DND and approves with
   a recorded comment + audit.
8. **MTN AI + Revenue & Inventory Intelligence** — under-utilised inventory,
   revenue by family/industry, projected revenue, opportunities; and **Inventory
   & Ad Formats** governance where MTN turns capabilities on/off per network.
9. **Approved campaign + Analytics** — advertiser sees "Approved by MTN Nigeria"
   and deterministic performance analytics.

## Honesty guardrails
- All metrics are **Demonstration data**; named brands are demonstration
  scenarios, not customers.
- Only LIVE/PILOT capabilities are shown as available on MTN; the rest read
  "Network Enablement Required".
- All "AI" is deterministic demonstration intelligence.
